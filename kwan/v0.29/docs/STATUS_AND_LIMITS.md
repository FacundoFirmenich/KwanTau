# KwanTau2DOS v0.29 — status and limits

## Demonstrated

- material activation of a future-only real prospective campaign;
- exact SHA-256 binding of the authorising USER turn;
- structural exclusion of that pre-freeze turn from case eligibility;
- post-freeze USER-turn candidate detection;
- fresh case-by-case `APPROVE_CASE`, `REJECT_CASE` or `DEFER` decision;
- separate case admission, campaign binding, mission binding and repair-trial eligibility;
- zero real cases produces `NO_EMPIRICAL_CLAIM`;
- no automatic authority, memory, training or promotion;
- source, clean ZIP and bundle-clone logical equivalence.

## Not demonstrated

- prospective benefit for the user;
- any real future case;
- eight or more balanced real cases;
- independent blind review;
- arm-specific real outcome quality;
- causal contribution of each context arm;
- production repair execution;
- `ACTIVE` readiness or scientific superiority.

## Constitutional boundary

```text
activation authorisation ≠ case consent
case consent ≠ case admission
case admission ≠ execution
repair eligibility ≠ repair execution
zero cases ≠ empirical evidence
```

# KWAN in KwanTau

This subtree is the governed integration surface for the canonical **KWAN** ecosystem inside `FacundoFirmenich/KwanTau`.

## Canon

- **Kwan Code Harness (KCH)** — constitutional authority, leases, receipts and abstention.
- **KwanTau DOS** — KwanTau Dual Operational System, the operational closure of KCH over Ride, state, resources, surfaces and time.
- **No run. No implant. Ride.**
- **KwanMe** — strictly personal sovereignty: personalisation, secrets, encrypted private documents, identity, recovery and governed disclosure.
- **KwanDocs** — document structure and workflows; private payload custody remains in KwanMe.
- **KwanBlocks** — additive content-addressed evidence and relation graph, immutable succession, preserved conflicts, authority ceiling `NONE`.
- **Sweetbox** — SHADOW execution and experimental evidence.
- **CreaLabs** — experimental composition and preregistration; composition is not execution authority.
- **Country World** — explicit territorial/jurisdictional relations with evidence, uncertainty and bitemporality.
- **Kwan Browser** — primary Chromium surface, not KwanTau DOS itself.
- **Observer–Jarvis** — observe, interpret and propose; never self-authorise.
- **Truqueplace** — governed exchange; verification never mints KCH authority.

In Spanish, KWAN-developed architectural centra are called **centrum**; in English, **core** is acceptable. The Spanish term `núcleo` is not canonical for KWAN-developed components.
